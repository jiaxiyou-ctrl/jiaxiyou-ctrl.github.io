03 / The next question

Can verification guide the next evidence decision?

The current pipeline passes evidence forward. A next step is to use feedback from the answer to guide what the system retrieves, protects or checks next.

The clearest result is improved citation support. Preserving answer completeness while reducing unsupported content remains open.

Evaluation covers public benchmarks and a research prototype. Evolving document versions, access permissions and live enterprise workloads require further study.
